About National Interiors

National Interiors is a Kattumannarkoil-based team working across UPVC doors and windows, modular kitchens, bedroom wardrobes, TV units, UPVC partitions, stainless steel and toughened glass handrails, and general interior works.

We work with quality materials and keep our pricing reasonable, so that good fittings and finishing don't have to come with a long wait or a difficult process. Every project is measured on site, planned around the space, and installed by our own team — from a single UPVC window to a full interior fit-out.

We serve homes and small businesses in and around Kattumannarkoil, and we're happy to visit your site, understand what you need, and give you a straightforward quotation.
